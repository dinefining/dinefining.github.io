Dinefining is a creative coder based in Cologne. Through live-coded music and visuals, he treats code as both instrument and medium, bringing layered algorithmic patterns together with storytelling and spontaneity to serve gourmet sounds, sometimes even baked beats on toast.

His work often sits in the grey spaces between dreams, melancholia, and a nostalgia for a future suspended somewhere between present and past. His soundscapes connect people to themselves and to the spaces they occupy. He sees code-based art as a practice of constraint: a form of expression born from limits, in a world where abundance is a privilege.

Dinefining is the creative alias of Ravi Popat. Drawing on a multidisciplinary background in engineering, film, visual communication, and design strategy, he works by day as a brand and service designer.
